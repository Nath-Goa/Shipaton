# Markva — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 September 2026_

Markva is an educational personal-finance app that combines simulated ("paper") stock trading with an expense tracker. All trading in Markva is simulated. The app does not connect to a real brokerage or a real bank account, does not execute real trades, and never handles real money or real investments.

This policy explains what information Markva handles, where it goes, and what choices you have.

Markva is independently developed. You can reach the developers at nthn.dev7@gmail.com.

## 1. Information that stays on your device

Most of what you do in Markva never leaves your phone. The following is stored locally in the app's own storage and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else:

- Expenses, budgets, savings goals and recurring schedules you create
- Your simulated portfolios, trades, watchlist and simulated cash balance
- Learning progress: quiz and flashcard results, streaks and badges
- App settings, including theme, notification preferences, and any AI provider API key you choose to enter
- Receipt and product photos you capture or select, and the details read from them
- Price-predictor learning data, if you turn that option on during onboarding

We do not run a server that receives, stores or backs up any of this. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed with it.

## 2. Information sent to third parties

Some features work by sending data to outside services. Each one is listed below, with what is sent and why.

**AI features** (Assistant chat, spending insights, receipt scanning, product scanning, quiz and lesson generation, headline explanations)
When you use an AI feature, the content that feature needs is sent to an AI provider so a response can be generated. Depending on your settings, that provider is Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, or OpenRouter. What is sent may include the message you typed, a summary of the expenses or portfolio the feature is describing, and — for receipt or product scanning — the photo you selected. These providers process the request under their own privacy policies. Markva sends nothing to an AI provider unless you use a feature that requires it.

**Market data** (Twelve Data, Yahoo Finance)
When you view a stock, the app requests price history and quotes for that ticker symbol. Only the symbol is sent. No personal or financial information about you is included.

**News** (Yahoo Finance)
The News tab and related features request headlines for ticker symbols. Only the symbol is sent.

**Optional account for Friends, Families and Duels** (Supabase)
Markva does not require an account. Every feature except Friends, Families and Duels works fully without signing in. If you choose to create an account for those features, we store your email address, a display name you choose, your friend and family relationships, and — for a duel — the percentage change in your simulated portfolio value. Your password is handled by Supabase and is never visible to us. We never store or transmit real financial information, because the app does not have any.

**Subscriptions** (RevenueCat and Google Play Billing)
If you buy a subscription, your purchase and subscription status is processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat so the app knows which features to unlock. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google and are never seen by Markva.

**Crash reporting** (Sentry)
Technical diagnostic information about a crash — the error itself, your device model and operating system version — is sent to Sentry so the problem can be fixed. This never includes your expenses, your portfolio or your messages.

As with any internet connection, the services above necessarily receive your device's IP address in order to respond.

## 3. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Markva contains no third-party advertising.

## 4. Permissions

- **Camera** — used only when you choose to photograph a receipt or a product.
- **Photos and media** — used only when you choose to select an existing image.
- **Notifications** — used for reminders you switch on, such as a daily check-in or a bill due tomorrow. These are generated on your device.
- **Biometrics (fingerprint or face)** — used only to unlock the app when you turn on App Lock. Biometric data is handled entirely by your device's operating system. It is never received by, or transmitted to, Markva.

You can revoke any of these in your device settings; the related feature will simply stop working.

## 5. Your choices, and how to delete your data

**On-device data** — delete individual items inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of it at once.

**AI features** — leave them unused, or enter your own API key in Settings so requests go through your own provider account rather than a shared one.

**Predictor learning** — decline it during onboarding, or reset it later in Settings.

**Account deletion** — if you created an account for Friends, Families or Duels and want it deleted, email nthn.dev7@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will delete the account, your profile, your friend and family relationships, and your duel records within 30 days of the request.

## 6. Children

Markva is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## 7. Security

Data sent to the services listed above travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. On-device data is held in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date above will change when we do, and material changes will be noted in the app.
